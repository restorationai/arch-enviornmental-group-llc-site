## Who do you call first, a tester or a contractor?

Call an independent testing company first whenever you need an unbiased answer: a pre-listing disclosure, a renovation permit, or a plain question about a peeling windowsill. Call an abatement or renovation contractor only after a test has confirmed lead is actually present and told you exactly where.

That order matters. A contractor who scopes the removal job has an obvious interest in finding something to remove. An independent tester, like Arch, has none. The [XRF lead paint testing](/services/lead-paint-testing/) reading you get back is usable with whatever licensed, unaffiliated contractor you choose, since the report isn't tied to a repair quote.

If the result does come back positive, you'll need a qualified lead abatement or EPA RRP-certified renovation contractor to do the actual work. Arch isn't that contractor, and that's the point: once the work is finished, bring in an independent clearance test, not performed by the crew that did the job, to confirm the space is safe before anyone moves back in.

## Do Fresno landlords and renovators need a lead test first?

Federal law requires owners of housing built before 1978 to disclose any known lead-based paint hazards to tenants before a new lease, but it doesn't force an owner to test. A test is the only reliable way to know what, if anything, there is to disclose.

Many Fresno landlords with pre-1978 units, especially ones with turnover scuffing or chipped trim, test proactively rather than guess. See [EPA's lead program](https://www.epa.gov/lead) for the federal framework behind the disclosure rule.

Renovation is a separate trigger. If the home was built before 1978 and the project disturbs more than a small area of painted surface, [EPA's Renovation, Repair and Painting Rule](https://www.epa.gov/lead/lead-renovation-repair-and-painting-program) requires the contractor to follow work practices. A test before the permit is pulled tells both you and the contractor whether that rule actually applies. Many of these same older Fresno properties also turn up asbestos in floor tile mastic or textured ceilings, so if the renovation touches both materials it's worth asking about [asbestos testing](/services/asbestos-testing/) at the same visit.
